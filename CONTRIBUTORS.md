## Upstream foundation and third party work

KM Companion began as a fork of the Smogon damage calculator and retains the work of its original authors and contributors. Those people, repositories, libraries, data sources, and license boundaries are documented in [Credits](CREDITS.md), the [retained MIT notice](LICENSES/MIT-legacy.txt), [Dependency Notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), and the [upstream contributor history](https://github.com/smogon/damage-calc/graphs/contributors).

ROM hack profiles and trainer packs also carry their own author, source, version, and redistribution metadata. Bundling a community contribution requires preserving that attribution in the data package and public credits.
