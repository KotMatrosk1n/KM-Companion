    AI Battle Simulator Guide
    
            
    
                
            
    
        
    
        
    
            
    Write a small battle log and Calculate to build a local timeline from temporary cloned Player and Trainer battle state.
    
            
    
                
    Data Source
    
                
    Player commands use Pokemon from the visible Player Team. Trainer commands use the selected Trainer roster. The simulator does not guess missing sets or pull from hidden selector state.
    
                
    The cloned simulation data is temporary. Closing the simulator clears those background battle objects, while your script and timeline stay visible until you clear them.
    
                
    Trainer AI runs locally inside the app with bundled Sword/Shield battle AI data. The simulator uses that data for move scoring and switch decisions without requiring external scripts.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    Syntax
    
                
    
                    
    Fight startReset active battle state.
    
                    
    Turn 1Start a turn and reset action order tracking.
    
                    
    Player Heracross inSet the active Player Pokemon.
    
                    
    Player Heracross [Power Herb] inSet or show the Player Pokemon's held item.
    
                    
    Trainer Rapidash inSet the active Trainer Pokemon.
    
                    
    Player switch to GastrodonSwitch the Player active Pokemon.
    
                    
    Trainer switch to RapidashSwitch the Trainer active Pokemon.
    
                    
    Rapidash uses Flame WheelCalculate the active Pokemon using a move.
    
                    
    Player Heracross faintsMark a Pokemon fainted and clear that side's active slot.
    
                    
    Weather Rain 5Set cloned weather and remaining turns.
    
                    
    Terrain Electric 5Set cloned terrain and remaining turns.
    
                    
    Player side: Stealth Rock, Spikes 2Set mapped Player side hazards.
    
                    
    Trainer side: Reflect 5, Tailwind 4Set mapped Trainer side conditions.
    
                    
    Field Trick Room 5Set an effect across the whole field and its remaining turns.
    
                    
    Clear Trainer sideClear mapped side conditions for that side.
    
                    
    Clear weatherClear cloned weather.
    
                    
    Clear terrainClear cloned terrain.
    
                
    
            
    
            
    
                
    Action Order
    
                
    in lines only set field state, similar to a Pokemon entering the active slot in a battle log, so both sides can enter before either side attacks. faints lines are also state updates, not turn actions.
    
                
    switch to and uses lines are turn actions and alternate by side. Use Turn 2, Turn 3, and so on to start a new turn when needed.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    Timeline
    
                
    Calculate groups the timeline into collapsed Setup and Turn sections. Open only the turns you want to inspect so long scripts stay readable.
    
                
    Move entries show Damage, HP, Risk, modeled effects, recharge requirements, AI move scores, and switch decisions when available.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    Top 3 Best Picks
    
                
    Trainer move entries can include a collapsible Top 3 Best Picks section. It ranks immediate Player choices, mixing active moves with switches instead of only listing switches.
    
                
    Move picks value KO chance, damage, speed, useful effects, and the likely next Trainer action. Switch picks value likely incoming damage, crit damage, survival turns, speed, return damage, entry effects, and whether the switch baits a favorable AI move.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    AI Scores
    
                
    AI scores show an expected score plus the possible score range. If the top score overlaps another move's range, the timeline marks the choice as contested instead of treating it as guaranteed.
    
                
    Craft a Line follows the top predicted trainer action and caches exact states while searching for a win.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    Field State
    
                
    Entry abilities such as Drought, Drizzle, Sand Stream, Snow Warning, Desolate Land, Primordial Sea, Delta Stream, Electric Surge, Grassy Surge, Misty Surge, and Psychic Surge update cloned weather or terrain. Remaining turns tick down on each new Turn line.
    
                
    Trainer AI reads cloned weather, terrain, Trick Room action order, Gravity, Magic Room, Wonder Room, hazards, screens, Tailwind, Safeguard, Mist, Sticky Web, Toxic Spikes, and bundled Sw/Sh side condition slots when scoring moves and switches.
    
                
    Guaranteed move effects apply to the cloned battle state. Chance effects are shown with their percent chance and contribute to AI evaluation, but they are not applied as guaranteed outcomes.
    
                
    Charge moves such as Sky Attack, Meteor Beam, Solar Beam, Fly, Dig, Dive, Bounce, Phantom Force, Skull Bash, Geomancy, Freeze Shock, Ice Burn, Razor Wind, Shadow Force, Sky Drop, and Solar Blade keep stored charge state. Power Herb resolves the move immediately and is then consumed; Solar Beam and Solar Blade skip charging in Sun or Harsh Sunshine.
    
                
    Recharge moves such as Eternabeam force the user to recharge on its next action, even after a KO.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    Craft A Line
    
                
    Craft a Line searches local Player and Trainer permutations for a winning script. It considers damaging moves, switches, speed, AI consistency, recharge, entry abilities, sacrifices to deal partial damage, stat drops, status pressure, accuracy drops, and guaranteed KOs.
    
                
    Craft assigns items only from the imported Player held item inventory, respects available item counts, and writes those items in brackets next to the Pokemon name. If no held item inventory is available, Craft stops instead of generating an itemless sandbox line.
    
                
    Try For No Losses prioritizes the fewest Player faints, Default balances losses with risk and speed, and Win At Any Cost prioritizes finding a win without protecting against sacrifices or roll risk.
    
                
    Found lines are sorted from most consistent to least consistent. Selecting a line replaces the Script box so you can Calculate and inspect it turn by turn.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    Shortcuts
    
                
    Use the autocomplete list while typing to insert valid commands faster. Drag a sprite from Player Team or Trainer Team into the Script box to insert the matching side's active or switch line.
